A QUICK GUIDE

How to use BoardExamTracker

01

Find your examination

Search by profession name or a familiar exam acronym, then narrow the list with date and category filters. Older announcements appear as you load more results.

02

Read the card details

The release date is when the announcement was posted. It can differ from the exam date. The short summary helps identify the notice; it is not a replacement for the source documents.

03

Check the result documents

An announcement may include a complete result, a school performance report, or a list of successful examinees. Open the relevant file and verify important details with the source.

BoardExamTracker is an independent index. PRC.gov.ph remains the authoritative source for examination results and updates.

READING A RESULT

What the result details tell you

DATE

Release date

This is the date the announcement appeared in the archive. It is different from the date candidates sat for the examination, which may be stated inside the notice.

COUNT

Reported pass figures

When the announcement gives a number of successful examinees and a total, our brief restates those figures to help you scan. The official documents remain the place to confirm names and individual outcomes.

FILES

Documents attached to a notice

Some notices include separate files for the complete result, school performance, or successful examinees. We display files linked by the announcement; availability and labels can vary by notice.

Our summaries are original, concise descriptions based on selected facts. They are not the official announcement, and they may omit context or later corrections.

ABOUT THE TRACKER

Built to make result day a little easier.

BoardExamTracker is an independent website designed to make it easier to find and track Philippine professional licensure examination results. We organize public announcements into a searchable archive, add short summaries written for quick scanning, and surface the documents attached to each notice.

Announcement titles, dates, reported figures, and linked files come from PRC. Our navigation, summaries, explanations, and tracking tools are BoardExamTracker content. We do not reproduce the full announcement text as our own.
